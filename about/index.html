About BGMC Technologies

A consultancy for businesses that want useful change, not AI theatre.

BGMC Technologies is a founder-led UK AI consultancy with enterprise software engineering experience. We help small and medium-sized businesses adopt AI in practical, affordable ways.

Book a Free AI Consultation Explore our services

We believe good AI consultancy starts with careful listening and sound judgement.

Business owners often know that time is being wasted, but do not know which technology would help. That is a sensible place to start. Our role is to understand the work, identify the real bottleneck and recommend an improvement that is proportionate to the value.

We are not here to sell AI for its own sake. If a process change or simple automation is the better answer, that is what we will recommend.

Our enterprise software engineering experience helps us look beyond the initial idea. We consider reliability, usability, access, accountability and how a change will fit into the working day.

The result is practical advice for SMEs, delivered without unnecessary complexity or exaggerated claims.

How delivery works

Clients buy BGMC's expertise and accountability.

Engineering delivery may involve our team and trusted implementation partners selected for the work. They operate under BGMC's direction. The consultancy and client relationship remain with BGMC.

  • BGMC leads business discovery and defines the problem
  • BGMC recommends and designs the solution
  • BGMC manages implementation and quality
  • BGMC remains the client's accountable point of contact

What stays connected
