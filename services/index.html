01 / Save time

AI workflow automation

For recurring work that is taking too much staff time, creating delays or relying on manual handovers.

The business problem

Information is copied between systems, routine work waits in inboxes and staff spend time checking, sorting or chasing the next step.

How AI helps

AI can read, classify, summarise and prepare information as part of a controlled workflow. Straightforward automation can then move approved work between the systems your team already uses.

Typical outcomes

  • Less time spent on repetitive administration
  • Faster, more consistent handovers
  • Fewer missed steps and less rekeying
  • More time for customer and judgement-led work

Who remains in control

Your team sets the rules. Sensitive outputs, unusual cases and important decisions can require approval, with clear escalation whenever the system is uncertain.

Not sure which workflow to start with? We can help you compare the effort, risk and likely value before you commit to a build.

02 / Find answers

Internal knowledge assistants

For teams that lose time searching for information or repeatedly asking experienced colleagues the same questions.

The business problem

Useful knowledge sits across documents, shared drives, policies, notes and the heads of senior staff. Finding a reliable answer takes too long.

How AI helps

A knowledge assistant can help colleagues ask questions in plain English and receive answers grounded in approved business information, with links back to the source.

Typical outcomes

  • Faster access to trusted internal information
  • Fewer interruptions for experienced staff
  • More consistent answers across the team
  • Smoother onboarding for new colleagues

Who remains in control

You decide which sources are approved and who can access them. The assistant is designed to show its sources, respect permissions and hand uncertain questions to a person.

We can help you assess whether your information is ready for an assistant and identify the smallest useful starting point.

03 / Keep data current

AI and agentic CRM systems

For teams that need their CRM to stay useful as conversations, decisions and follow-ups move through the business.

The business problem

Email history sits outside the CRM, contact records go stale and important context is lost between conversations, handovers and follow-ups.

How AI helps

AI can fetch and sync relevant email activity, keep records and next steps up to date, understand the relationship context and use approved knowledge stores to prepare or send appropriate responses.

Typical outcomes

  • More current CRM records without constant admin
  • A clearer view of conversation history and commitments
  • Faster, more consistent responses grounded in approved information
  • Follow-ups and next steps surfaced before they are missed

Who remains in control

You decide which sources can be synced, what the system can change and when it can act. Automatic responses can be limited to agreed scenarios, with uncertain or sensitive conversations routed to a person.

If your team works across email and a CRM but context keeps getting lost, we can map the data, knowledge and action rules needed for a useful starting point.

04 / Work on the move

Mobile workflow apps

For teams that need the same CRM context, knowledge and next actions wherever the working day takes them.

The business problem

Important work happens away from a desk, but the useful context stays in the office. Updates wait, notes are lost and mobile teams have to rely on memory or disconnected tools.

How AI helps

A mobile app can bring the right records, email context, tasks and approved knowledge to the phone. AI can summarise what matters, surface the next step and help prepare actions while the conversation is still fresh.

Typical outcomes

  • Updates captured while work is happening
  • Conversation history and knowledge available on the move
  • Follow-ups recorded before they are forgotten
  • Office and mobile teams working from the same current information

Who remains in control

Mobile access follows your existing permissions and approval rules. Actions are visible and accountable, and automatic changes or responses can be restricted to the scenarios you agree.

If important work moves between the desk, site and customer, we can design the mobile entry point that keeps your workflow connected.
