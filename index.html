Common questions

Start with a conversation, not a technical brief.

You do not need to know what to build or how AI fits. That is what the discovery process is for.

What happens in the free AI consultation?

We discuss how your business currently works, where time is being lost and what you would like to improve. It is a practical, no-obligation conversation. You do not need to prepare a technical brief.

What if AI is not the right answer?

We will say so. A simpler automation, process change or conventional software solution may be more suitable. Our advice is based on the business problem, not a preference for a particular technology.

Will our team remain in control?

Yes. We design clear approvals, escalation routes and access controls around the way your team works. Sensitive decisions and exceptions can always remain with the right person.

Who designs and delivers the solution?

BGMC leads discovery, solution design and project management. Delivery may involve our engineering team and trusted implementation partners, all working under BGMC's direction and accountability.

How much does a project cost?

The initial consultation is free. If there is a worthwhile opportunity, we will define the scope and provide a clear proposal before any work begins. Recommendations are designed to be proportionate to the value for your business.
